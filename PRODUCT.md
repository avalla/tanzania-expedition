# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Potential donors (friends, network, strangers deciding whether to trust the cause), existing supporters who followed Drops in the Ocean and want updates and proof, and press or partners who need credible facts fast. Visitors often arrive from social media on a phone, in four languages (IT, EN, ES, FR). The job: understand the mission, believe the team, and donate (IBAN, shirts, benefit party).

## Product Purpose

Official site of Tanzania Expedition, an independent humanitarian expedition supporting Swahili and Masai communities in Tanzania. The next goal is a borehole well near Kimotorok, giving water to people, livestock, cooking, washing and hygiene. Success: visitors trust the team, feel the real need for water, and fund the well. Content must stay strictly factual; nothing is invented beyond the supplied sources.

## Brand Personality

Documentary, grassroots, accountable. Honest field voice: warm, direct, hand-made, never slick. Emotion is trust plus urgency: the need is real and the team keeps its promise to return.

## Anti-references

- Poverty-porn and savior tropes (pity imagery, sad-child close-ups, white-savior framing).
- Corporate NGO templates (stock photos, blue gradients, generic "Donate now" banners).
- Safari-tourism clichés (sunset silhouettes, acacia, Big Five aesthetics).
- Crowdfunding-startup polish (SaaS cards, gradients, glass).

## Design Principles

1. **Dignity over pity.** People are shown as neighbors and partners, never as objects of sympathy.
2. **Proof, not polish.** Real field photography, dates, places and sources earn trust; decoration does not.
3. **Say less, say it true.** Every claim traces to a supplied source; copy is plain and specific.
4. **One clear path to give.** Donation info is always findable without pressure or dark patterns.
5. **Four languages, one voice.** Layouts survive IT/EN/ES/FR length differences and low-end phones.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard and visible focus, sufficient contrast, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, readable and fast on low-end mobile connections, correct `lang` and translated alt text per language.
